Kudoly

Reward the people you cheer on.

Kudoly turns everyday effort into something worth keeping. Give the people you support — your kids, students, teammates, or clients — a real, collectible medal for what they accomplish: hikes, bike rides, household chores, or any activity you define.

Recipients open a sealed case, the medal bursts out, and they can flip it over to read who gave it, what for, and when. Medals sync privately through iCloud and appear on iPhone and Apple Watch.

What makes it nice

  • Steel, bronze, silver, and gold medals for any activity
  • Built-in activities plus custom ones with your own tiers and units
  • A delightful unboxing reveal every time
  • iPhone and Apple Watch, kept in sync
  • Private by design — your data lives in your own iCloud
